project with OpenAI's built-in image-generation model on 2026-07-30. It does
not contain Mojang's wordmark, a Minecraft grass block, a creeper face, or
another launcher's branding.

> Create a polished original Android launcher app icon for an app named
> MCLauncher. Square 1:1 composition, no text and no wordmark. A bold,

> ribbons that subtly suggest the letters M and C and form a small forward-play

> lavender-purple and fresh emerald-teal accents matching a modern dark
> launcher UI. Crisp vector-like edges, restrained 3D depth, high contrast,
> professional software brand identity, centered with generous padding inside
> the Android adaptive-icon safe zone, readable at 48 px. Do not include
> Minecraft's logo, a grass block, a creeper face, Mojang branding, characters,

resources. No external launcher or game artwork was used as a reference.
